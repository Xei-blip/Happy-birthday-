i hope this year is kinder to you.

and when life gets heavy, i hope you remember how far you’ve already come.

there were probably moments when you thought you wouldn’t make it through certain things, but somehow, you did. you’re still here. still laughing. still dreaming. still becoming the person you’re meant to be. and honestly, i think that’s something worth being proud of.

you deserve good things, not because you’ve earned them perfectly, but simply because you’re you.

thank you for existing. thank you for the memories. thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every random moment, and every little thing you probably didn’t realize meant something to me. some moments may have seemed ordinary to you, but i’ll always be grateful that i got to experience them with you.

and one day, when you look back at this version of yourself, i hope you smile.

i hope you’re proud of the person you became.

so today, forget about everything you think you should have already accomplished. forget about the things that didn’t work out. forget about the people who hurt you. just for today, let yourself be celebrated.

because you deserve that.

happy birthday, genuinely. i hope this year gives you countless reasons to smile, memories you’ll never want to forget, and people who remind you just how loved you are.

i’m really, really glad you’re here.

happy birthday. ❤️
